3. What you should notice

Phishing warning signs

  • Urgency: threats such as “today” or “account restricted” push you to act quickly.
  • Unexpected message: don't trust a message just because it uses a support-style name or logo.
  • Unknown link: check the full domain before opening it; look for misspellings and extra words.
  • Credential request: never enter your password or OTP after following an unexpected message link.
  • Verify independently: open the official app yourself and check its in-app notifications.
